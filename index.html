What I believe

The ideas I live and work by

Change is the measure of intelligence.

I have reinvented myself again and again, from software engineering to quality, innovation, strategy and research. I choose the unknown over the comfortable, because that is where growth happens.

Reimagine, don't just automate.

Putting today's process into new technology wastes the technology. I start from how the work should be done, then decide what the tools are for.

People first: lift them up, don't replace them.

Technology only changes the world when it fits into the fabric of human experience. Progress should move people up to better work.

Ten times is possible.

People and organisations can achieve far more than they think, and spend that energy on the problems that matter for the human race.

Leave the planet better.

I care about children, education and the environment, from tree-planting drives to protecting a national park in Mumbai from encroachment.

Think sideways.

As a certified De Bono lateral-thinking instructor and Six Sigma Master Black Belt, I pair creativity with rigour: wild ideas, measured results.
